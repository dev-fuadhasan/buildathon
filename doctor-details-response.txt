About Asst. Prof. Dr. Sharmin Akter Liza
----------------------------------------

Asst. Prof. Dr. Sharmin Akter Liza is one of the best gynecologist and infertility specialist in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Her qualification is MBBS, FCPS (OBGYN).
